How to choose an event manager in Sambalpur

  • Ask what they will handle: vendor booking, on-day coordination, budget tracking, or all three.
  • Look at two or three events they have managed of a similar size and style.
  • Clarify their fee structure: flat fee, percentage of budget, or a hybrid.
  • Get the scope of work and the deliverable list in writing before you pay anything.
  • Ask for a post-event report or debrief if you have booked their full planning service.
  • For events near Hirakud or Burla, ask vendors about travel distance and set-up logistics.

What does an event manager do?

An event manager plans and coordinates the event: sourcing vendors, tracking budget, planning the schedule, running rehearsals and managing the day itself so the host can focus on guests.

How much does an event manager cost in Sambalpur?

Fees vary by scope and event size. Many charge a flat fee for smaller events, or a percentage of the total budget for large weddings. Ask for a written scope so the cost matches what is delivered.

Do I need an event manager for a small wedding?

Not always. If your vendor count is small and you have family to help, a coordinator for the day itself may be enough. For larger weddings with multiple functions, a manager is usually worth it.
