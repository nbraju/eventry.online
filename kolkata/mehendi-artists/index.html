How to choose a mehendi artist in Kolkata

  • Ask for photos of full bridal mehendi the artist has done, not just guest designs.
  • Confirm the number of artists coming. A single artist cannot cover a bride plus 50 guests in one evening.
  • Agree on the time slot for the bride: bridal mehendi often takes two to four hours, sometimes more.
  • Ask about aftercare advice and any prep the bride should do beforehand.
  • Bring extra artists if the family also wants mehendi on the same day.
  • Confirm venue rules on outside caterers and decorators well in advance; some community halls have fixed vendor panels.

How early should I book a mehendi artist for a Kolkata wedding?

For peak wedding dates, book one to three months ahead. Popular bridal artists fill up fast, especially in wedding season. For smaller functions, a few weeks is often enough.

How many mehendi artists do I need?

One for the bride plus one per twenty to thirty guests is a reasonable starting point. If many guests want mehendi, book extra artists and start early.

How long does bridal mehendi take?

Full bridal mehendi on hands and feet can take two to four hours, sometimes more for very detailed designs. Leave the bride free for the afternoon and schedule the artist to arrive with time to spare.
