Ideal Inventory per Event Type

🎉 Wedding
  • Furniture: Chairs x300+, round tables x30, sofa sets x8
  • Tent: Shamiyana 40×40 ft or larger
  • Lighting: Stage lights, LED uplights, fairy lights
  • Decor: Flower arrangements, drapes, centrepieces
  • Catering: Buffet setup, serving stations, crockery for 500+
🏢 Corporate
  • Furniture: Conference chairs x100, modular stages
  • AV: LCD projector, large screen, wireless mics x4
  • Lighting: Stage wash, LED video wall
  • Catering: Breakfast/tea stations, lunch buffet
🎂 Birthday / Social
  • Furniture: Chairs x80, round tables x10
  • Decor: Balloon arches, theme backdrops
  • Catering: Snack counters, cake table
  • Lighting: Fairy lights, disco ball
🏟️ Exhibition
  • Furniture: Modular booths, display tables x50
  • Lighting: Track lights, spotlights, LED strips
  • AV: Digital signage, screens, PA system

Disaster Recovery (Plan B)

Rain / Bad weather — Backup indoor venue
Power outage — Generator + battery lights
Provider no-show — Standby list of 2–3 providers
Medical emergency — First-aid kit + hospital contact
