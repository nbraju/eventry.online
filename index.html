Ideal Inventory per Event Type

Use these checklists to ensure you have the right gear, furniture, and supplies for your event.

🎉 Wedding

  • Furniture: Chairs x300+, round tables x30, sofa sets x8, mandap/altar
  • Tent: Shamiyana 40×40 ft or larger, side walls, flooring
  • Lighting: Stage lights, LED uplights, fairy lights, spotlights
  • Decor: Flower arrangements, drapes, centrepieces, aisle decor
  • Catering: Buffet setup, serving stations, crockery for 500+
  • AV: Sound system, wireless mics, projector, screen

🏢 Corporate

  • Furniture: Conference chairs x100, modular stages, registration desks
  • AV: LCD projector, large screen, wireless mics x4, speakers
  • Lighting: Stage wash, LED video wall, spotlights
  • Catering: Breakfast/tea stations, lunch buffet, water dispensers
  • Decor: Branded backdrops, step-and-repeat, table covers
  • Stationery: Name badges, notepads, pens, event agendas

🎂 Birthday / Social

  • Furniture: Chairs x80, round tables x10, kids' tables, lounge seating
  • Decor: Balloon arches, theme backdrops, centrepieces, banners
  • Catering: Snack counters, cake table, drink station
  • Lighting: Fairy lights, disco ball, photo booth lights
  • Entertainment: DJ system, karaoke, games area

🏟️ Exhibition / Trade

  • Furniture: Modular booths, display tables x50, chairs x200
  • Lighting: Track lights, spotlights, LED strips
  • AV: Digital signage, screens, PA system
  • Decor: Branded banners, floor graphics, reception counters
  • Logistics: Power distribution, extension boards, Wi-Fi hotspots

Pro tip: Always add 15–20% extra chairs & tableware for unexpected guests.

Standard Operating Procedures

Follow these SOPs to ensure every event runs smoothly — from planning to teardown.

Phase 1: Planning & Sourcing

  1. Define event brief — guest count, date, location, budget, theme, and must-have vendors.
  2. Vendor shortlisting — use Eventry to find 3–5 vendors per category. Check ratings and portfolio.
  3. Enquiry & site visits — send enquiries, visit top 2–3 venues, taste catering samples, review decor mockups.
  4. Finalise & sign — compare quotes, negotiate, sign agreements with clear scope, payment terms, and cancellation policy.

Phase 2: Pre-event Coordination

  1. Run-through meeting — call all vendors 7 days before. Confirm timelines, load-in times, power points, parking.
  2. Floor plan & run sheet — share layout with vendors. Create a minute-by-minute run sheet for the event day.
  3. Final headcount — confirm final guest count 72 hours before — this locks catering and seating.
  4. Weather check — for outdoor events, have a backup tent/indoor plan ready.

Phase 3: Event Day Execution

  1. Vendor check-in — welcome vendors at load-in. Verify deliveries against checklist.
  2. Quality control — inspect setup: tables, linens, lighting, sound check, floral arrangement.
  3. Guest flow — manage arrival, seating, food service, and program transitions with a floor manager.
  4. Vendor payment — process final payments as per agreement — cash or UPI on the day.

Phase 4: Teardown & Follow-up

  1. Supervised teardown — ensure vendors pack out safely. Check for damaged items and document.
  2. Final walkthrough — inspect venue for damage, lost items, and cleanliness.
  3. Vendor feedback — rate vendors on Eventry — helps the community and builds your credibility.
  4. Thank you notes — send a short thank-you to all vendors and guests. Build lasting relationships.

How to Choose the Right Vendor

Must-have

  • Active listing — verify on Eventry with live contact
  • Recent portfolio — ask for photos from last 3 events
  • Transparent pricing — no hidden charges
  • Insurance — for venues, AV, and high-value items
  • Responsive — replies within 4 hours

Nice-to-have

  • Local presence — reduces travel and logistics costs
  • Multiple packages — flexibility to scale up/down
  • Referrals — past client testimonials
  • Sustainability — eco-friendly options
  • Backup equipment — on-site spares

Disaster Recovery (Plan B)

Unexpected issues happen. Have these backup plans ready:

Rain / Bad weather
Backup indoor venue + extra tents & flooring
Power outage
Generator + extra extension cords & battery lights
Vendor no-show
Maintain a standby list of 2–3 vendors per category
Medical emergency
First-aid kit + nearest hospital contact
Sound / AV failure
Have a spare speaker & mic on standby
Staff shortage
On-call event volunteers or temp agency contacts
