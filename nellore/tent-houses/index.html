How to choose a tent house in Nellore

  • Visit the godown or ask for recent photos of the actual stock; tent fabric, chairs and carpets vary widely in condition.
  • Check weather cover. Waterproof roofing and a drainage plan are essential for the monsoon and for sudden showers.
  • Confirm fire-safety basics such as extinguisher placement and clear exits, especially with kitchens or lighting nearby.
  • Ask about damage and loss terms for chairs, carpets and decor items, and how any deposit is returned.
  • Compare vendors on a like-for-like item list rather than a single bundle price.
  • Coastal weather can turn quickly; ask for waterproof roofing and a drainage plan for any open-ground set-up.

How do I estimate the tent size for my guest count?

Tell the vendor how many guests will be seated and whether you want a stage, buffet counters or a dance floor. A seated-plus-buffet layout needs more space than seating alone, so the vendor will usually measure the venue and propose a layout.

Can a tent house also provide chairs, stage and lighting?

Many tent houses offer chairs, tables, stage and mandap structures, carpets and basic lighting under one quote. Ask for an itemised list so you can compare it with separate decorators or lighting vendors.

Is a deposit usually required when hiring a tent house?

Most vendors take an advance to block the date and sometimes a refundable security deposit for equipment. Ask for the amounts, the conditions for return and a receipt.
