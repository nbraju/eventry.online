How to choose a wedding vendor in Srikakulam

  • Ask each vendor for an itemised quote so you can compare like for like.
  • Share the same schedule with every vendor so set-up, food and rituals do not clash.
  • Name one family member as the point of contact for vendors on the day.
  • Keep a contingency reserve in the budget for changes and last-minute needs.
  • Visit the venue with the decorator, tent house and lighting team together if you can.
  • For events in small towns and villages, ask vendors about travel and set-up time; some may need to arrive a day early.

In what order should I book wedding vendors in Srikakulam?

Start with the venue and date, then the caterer and photographer for popular dates, followed by the decorator, tent house, lighting and sound, and entertainers. Vendors with limited capacity fill up first.

How do I compare quotes from different vendors?

Ask for itemised quotes built on the same assumptions: guest count, hours, services and staffing. Compare what is included, such as set-up, transport and overtime, before you compare totals.

Should I keep a contingency in the wedding budget?

Yes. A reserve for last-minute changes, extra guests and overtime is sensible. Decide its size by how certain your guest count and schedule are.
