How to choose an event decorator in Cuttack

  • Be clear on flowers: fresh, artificial or mixed. Fresh flower prices swing with season and demand around festival and wedding dates.
  • Visit the venue with the decorator to check ceiling height, power points, load-bearing points and set-up access.
  • Agree on the set-up and dismantling schedule, and who coordinates with the venue and with the tent or lighting team.
  • Ask what is rented and what you keep, such as props, backdrops and floral pieces.
  • Discuss a backup plan for weather or last-minute changes, especially for open-air events.
  • Cuttack and Bhubaneswar are close enough that many vendors serve both; ask which side of the river they are based on.

What does wedding decoration usually include?

A typical package covers the stage or mandap, entrance, photo backdrop and seating or aisle styling. Lighting, flowers and props may be quoted separately, so ask for an itemised list that lets you compare quotes fairly.

Should I choose fresh or artificial flowers?

Fresh flowers look and smell better but cost more and depend on seasonal supply. Artificial flowers last through long events and weather but can look flat in photographs. Many decorators combine the two.

How early should I finalise the decoration?

For large events, lock the theme and budget at least a month ahead so the decorator can source materials and plan the build. For peak dates, book earlier.
