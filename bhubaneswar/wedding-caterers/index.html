How to choose a wedding caterer in Bhubaneswar

  • Ask for a tasting of the exact menu items you plan to serve, not a general sample.
  • Fix the menu, the per-plate rate and the guest-count basis in writing, and ask how final billing treats guests above or below the estimate.
  • Check kitchen requirements: space, water, gas or power, and whether cooking happens on site or off site.
  • Confirm dietary needs early: pure vegetarian, Jain, no onion or garlic, diabetic-friendly and allergy requests.
  • Ask about food safety: ingredient sourcing, hygiene practices, and what happens to leftover food.
  • For events in the twin-city area around Cuttack, confirm which vendor network covers your venue.

How do I decide the per-plate budget for a wedding?

Start from the guest count and the number of meals, then compare quotes on a like-for-like menu: the same dishes, service style and staffing. A lower rate may leave out serving staff, plates or live counters.

How far in advance should I book a caterer?

For busy wedding dates, three to four months ahead is a sensible starting point. Smaller functions can often be arranged with a few weeks' notice. Book the tasting as soon as you have shortlisted two or three caterers.

Can a caterer manage both vegetarian and non-vegetarian menus?

Many can, but ask how they keep the two kitchens, utensils and counters separate. Confirm this in writing if your family or guests need strictly separate preparation.
