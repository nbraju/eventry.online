How to choose a wedding photographer in Guntur

  • Ask to see two or three complete wedding albums rather than a highlight reel; a full set shows consistency across lighting, crowds and long days.
  • Ask what happens if the lead photographer falls ill or the schedule overruns, and whether overtime is charged hourly or per function.
  • Share your muhurtham time and ritual list early so the team can plan positions for the key moments.
  • Discuss usage rights: whether the studio may share your images online and whether you receive full-resolution files.
  • Visit the venue together if you can, especially indoor halls where light is limited.
  • If your event is in a town around Guntur rather than the city itself, ask vendors about travel charges and whether the crew can arrive the day before.

How early should I book a wedding photographer in Guntur?

For dates in busy wedding months, start enquiring three to four months ahead; good teams fill up quickly around auspicious dates. For smaller functions a few weeks is often enough. Whatever the date, confirm availability and advance terms in writing.

What is the difference between candid and traditional wedding photography?

Traditional photography covers posed portraits and rituals in a planned sequence. Candid photography captures unposed moments as they happen. Many couples book both, so ask each photographer how the two are combined and priced.

What should I get in writing from a wedding photographer?

Dates and hours of coverage, the team on each day, deliverables with delivery timelines, overtime rules, the advance and balance schedule, cancellation terms and image-usage rights.
