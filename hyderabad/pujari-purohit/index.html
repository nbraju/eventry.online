How to choose a pujari in Hyderabad

  • Confirm the pujari knows your family's specific tradition and language.
  • Ask what samagri (materials) you need to arrange and what the pujari will bring.
  • Ask the pujari to explain the meaning of each step if family members are unfamiliar.
  • Confirm the fee and travel in writing, and whether it covers only the ceremony or also consultations.
  • Book well ahead during peak wedding months and festival dates.
  • Traffic across Hyderabad can change set-up timings sharply, so ask each vendor how they plan transport and crew arrival for your venue and date.
