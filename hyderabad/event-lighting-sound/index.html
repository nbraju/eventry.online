How to choose a lighting and sound vendor in Hyderabad

  • Share the venue size and event schedule so the vendor can size speakers, lights and power correctly.
  • Confirm how many technicians will be on site, not only the equipment list.
  • Schedule a sound check before guests arrive, including the microphones for speeches and rituals.
  • Ask for spares of critical items such as microphones, mixers and amplifiers.
  • Coordinate with the decorator on lighting accents so cables and trusses do not clash with the stage.
  • Traffic across Hyderabad can change set-up timings sharply, so ask each vendor how they plan transport and crew arrival for your venue and date.

Do I need a generator for my event?

If the venue's power supply is limited or unreliable, or you are using large lighting and LED set-ups, a generator is sensible. Ask the vendor to size it for the full load and to keep a technician on site.

Are there time limits for loudspeakers and DJs?

Local rules and venue policies often restrict amplified sound after certain hours. Check the rules with the venue and local authorities, then plan the schedule with your vendor accordingly.

What is the difference between a DJ and a sound system vendor?

A DJ performs and manages the music. A sound vendor supplies and operates the speakers, mixers and microphones. Some businesses offer both, so ask whether one quote covers equipment, operator and music.
